2. What each number means

  • Similarity (blue shape) is a rank, not a percent of sameness: the share of reference Earth windows that are further from the selected spot than this site in that category.
  • Mismatch (orange dashed line) = 1 − similarity. It shows the same information from the other side.
  • Match score: the weighted average of the category similarities. It is absolute: it is not stretched so that the best site gets 100%.
  • Categories compared: categories with data on both sides. Missing ones are left out, not counted as zero, and the remaining weights are rescaled.
